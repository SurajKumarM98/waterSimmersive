About

"Being Water" is a captivating mixed-reality adventure that transforms players into active explorers of Arizona's vital water systems. Players feel they are Being Water by using full-body movements to interactively summon clouds, unleash rain, and control water's journey through the desert landscape. A wise water spirit guides players in this immersive game, and children as young as 4 years-old can experience the journey while learning about water in the desert. Players use gestures and body movements, like raising and then lowering their arms, to cue the clouds to release rain. The game is displayed on a large 98” monitor, which makes it highly immersive and viewable by many museum exhibit visitors.

Development Time

1.5 Years

Team

Mina Johnson Glenberg, Claire Lauer, Steven Hernandez, Yanjun Lyu, Pranav Joshi, Sophia Baia, Nia Renee Cooper, Chloe Reyes, Hannah Bartolomea, Danielle Storey

Focus Areas

The water cycle, sustainable water in the desert, storytelling, embodied STEM learning

Learning Outcomes


  1. Understand Arizona's water sources, including rivers, lakes, groundwater, and aquifers.
  2. Recognize local water challenges specific to Arizona communities.
  3. Learn ways in which water can be conserved in the desert environment.
